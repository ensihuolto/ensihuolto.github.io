Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Data Controller

Eastern Uusimaa Emergency Support Services Unit privacy@ensihuolto.fi https://ensihuolto.fi

2. Data We Collect

We collect and process only the minimum personal data necessary to provide and secure our services. When you sign in through an external identity provider, we may receive your account identifier, email address, and basic profile information, depending on the provider and the permissions granted.

We do not request or access information beyond what is necessary for authentication and the operation of our services.

3. Use and Retention of Data

Personal data is used solely to provide, maintain, and secure the service. We retain data only for as long as necessary for these purposes or as required by applicable law. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

4. Cookies

We use only cookies that are strictly necessary for the operation and security of our services, such as those required for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Disclosure of Data

We do not disclose personal data to third parties unless required by applicable law or a legally binding order issued by a competent authority. Any legally required disclosure is limited to the information covered by that requirement.

6. Data Security and Your Rights

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. Under applicable data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data, restrict or object to its processing, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise your rights or ask questions about this policy, contact us using the details above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when necessary. The current version will be published on this page.
